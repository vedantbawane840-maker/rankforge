Frequently Asked Questions

How do Apify actor compute costs work?

RankForge operates on a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. We charge exactly $0 markup on web crawlers, SERP scrapers, or proxies. All compute runs directly on your Apify account with full transparency.

What payment methods are supported?

Subscriptions are securely handled globally through Dodo Payments, accepting Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay in over 135 currencies.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly through the dashboard with zero lock-in or cancellation fees.

What happens if I exceed my monthly audit limit?

When your monthly limit is reached, tools return a polite upgrade message to your AI agent. You can upgrade immediately to a higher tier, or wait for your quota to reset at the start of your billing cycle.
