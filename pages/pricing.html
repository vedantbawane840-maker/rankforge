Billing FAQs

What payment methods are supported?

Payments are securely handled via Dodo Payments supporting credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay globally.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly through the dashboard with zero lock-in.

How do Apify actor costs work?

RankForge charges $0 markup on Apify runs. You bring your own Apify key, and Apify bills you directly for standard actor compute.
